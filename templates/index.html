At the table

  1. Find a table

    Pick a category, or press / and search every table and prompt.

  2. Roll

    Click a table to roll it. Press R to roll again, or S to roll on any table.

  3. Keep the good ones

    Pin prompts for tonight's session. Your last 10 rolls stay in this browser.

  4. Make it yours

    Use Customize to hide tables or load your own, saved privately in your browser.

Also available as a JSON API: /api/structure, /api/random, /api/search?q=… and /api/combos.
